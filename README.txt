THE LAST SPARK - Godot 4.7.2 project

Open this folder in Godot and press F5 to play.

CONTROLS
- A / D or LEFT / RIGHT: walk backward / forward
- Double-tap D: toggle between walking and running
- SPACE / W / UP: jump; press again in the air to double-jump
- ESC: pause or resume
- R: restart at any time
- START GAME, RESUME, RESTART, and PLAY AGAIN are clickable and keyboard-focusable

Avoid spikes, falling rocks, moving creatures, and gaps. Clear traps without
touching them to grow the spark's light. Checkpoint lamps restore some light
and remember your progress. If the light fades completely, bounce back to the
last lamp. Reach the glowing orb to bring the forest back to life.

The forest is a longer 48,000-unit run (about 2-3 minutes at full speed), with
lamps roughly 4,000 units apart. Your spark does not drain with time: hits dim
it, while clean hazard passes and lamps restore it. The recovery keeps a visible
halo around the frog, and the end-of-game light-spread animation remains intact.
Traps become denser and more varied as you progress, with occasional paired
hazards. Eight raised parkour courses cross wide gaps; their ledges reward
careful timing and double jumps.

The Swamp of Digeridoos ambience plays in a soft loop throughout the game.

The Ninja Frog sprite sheets are imported losslessly without mipmaps and are
shown with nearest-neighbor filtering to keep their pixels crisp. The sprite
sheets are in assets/player/. The supplied Haven forest,
tree, rock, and spike assets are also used by the game.
